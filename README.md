# Pawgress

A cozy study companion by Moki and Mochi. No account, no tracking, no paywall. Everything is saved in your browser (localStorage).

## Features

**Focus**
- Study timer (15, 25, 45, 60 minutes or your own length) with pause, end early and a 5-minute break
- A chubby sticker companion that reads beside you, encourages you, and naps at night
- Earn acorns: 1 per 5 minutes, plus 2 for finishing a session

**Customize**
- 13 animals: cat, dog, bunny, bear, hamster, sheep, penguin, panda, fox, cow, mouse, owl and frog
- Breeds and coats for each (calico, tuxedo, Siamese, Shiba, Dalmatian, beagle, pug, red panda and more), picked from a ring of paws
- 9 room backgrounds with window weather (rain, sakura petals, snow, starry loft and more)
- Outfits (bow, glasses, scarf, headphones, beret) and room decor that grows as you study

**The Pawgress Garden**
- Unlocks after your first completed focus timer, and is locked during focus sessions
- Each completed timer earns a flower seed and grows every planted flower by one stage. Finishing a break earns a water drop for a little extra growth.
- Flowers never wilt. Pick blooms for your bouquet.
- 9 flowers to discover through study milestones, collected in a flower book

**Advent calendar**
- From mid-November, a gift button counts down to Christmas
- 25 doors, December 1 to 25, each with a special edition holiday flower or outfit
- Missed a day? Open it later. Holiday items stay yours forever.

**Sounds**
- Lo-fi music, white noise, ocean waves, rain and a fireplace, all generated live in the browser. Mix them with separate volumes.
- Paste your own Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud or Apple Music link to play it in the app, or link any music page

**Planner**
- 7-day strip, tasks with subjects and due dates, overdue list, and one tap to focus on a task

**Study cards**
- Decks of terms and definitions, added one at a time or pasted in bulk
- Flashcards with mastery tracking
- Practice tests that mix multiple choice, reverse multiple choice, true/false and typed answers

**Stats**
- Minutes today, day streak, total hours, sessions, tasks done, cards mastered
- Last-7-days chart, time by subject, recent test scores, and manual time logging

## Run it

It's a single HTML file with no build step. Either:

- open `index.html` in a browser, or
- serve the folder so offline mode and installing as an app work:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit http://localhost:8000.
